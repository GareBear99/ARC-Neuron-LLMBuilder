# Transitional License Roadmap

This repository is moving toward a protected 3.0 release strategy without rewriting the legal history of earlier releases.

## Canonical licensing facts

1. Historical 1.0 releases keep the license they shipped with.
2. There will not be a formal public 2.0 release.
3. The 1.0-to-3.0 development bridge may remain transitional while the protected 3.0 package is prepared.
4. The 3.0 release is intended to be the full roadmap/base-model release with connected datasets and clearer commercial restrictions.
5. The 3.0 license should protect the source, trained weights, manifests, candidate derivatives, benchmark reports, model branding, and commercial redistribution path.

## What the transitional notice does

The transitional notice makes it clear that the current package is not a blanket grant for unrestricted future 3.0 commercial resale or repackaging.

## What still requires legal review

Before 3.0, get legal review for:

- trained-weight license,
- dataset-source compatibility,
- contributor terms,
- SaaS wrapping restrictions,
- model derivative restrictions,
- trademark/name usage,
- redistribution rules,
- and commercial support terms.

## Non-destruction rule

Changing the future license must not rewrite the past. The release history must remain honest: old releases retain old terms, new protected releases use new terms.
